# Learning and Applying Reporting and Presentation Style

## Establish the Audience and Occasion

Establish the audience, occasion, purpose, format, and length or duration from the task. Distinguish weekly updates, milestone reports, decision requests, retrospectives, and presentations. Ask only for missing information that materially affects the result.

Prefer accepted examples and explicit revision comments for the same audience and occasion. Material written by a leader can show that person's expression habits, but does not by itself establish requirements for every report submitted to them.

## Learn Observable Preferences

- Information order: conclusions, progress, impact, evidence, and open decisions.
- Level of detail: length, technical depth, data density, and the treatment of risks and problems.
- Actionability: owners, dates, next steps, and explicit decision requests when expected.
- Presentation structure: information per slide, conclusion-led titles where supported, and the relationship between charts, notes, and spoken explanations.

Extract specific, actionable conventions and distinguish audience expectations from arrangements for a single event. Do not infer a leader's personality, psychology, or hidden preferences without evidence.

When asked to learn or save findings, follow the [knowledge guide](knowledge.md) and write to the target project's reporting topic, defaulting to `.team-context/reporting-style.md`. Scope findings by audience and occasion, and preserve uncertainty about unverified identities or acceptance.

## Complete the Deliverable

1. Gather actual progress, results, figures, risks, and requests for assistance from the task and verifiable sources. Include the necessary time range or measurement basis for figures, and leave unknown status unconfirmed.
2. Apply guidance matching the audience and occasion. Without enough examples, use a concise, clear structure and state that audience preferences remain unverified.
3. Adapt style in service of accurate communication. Do not hide risks, blockers, or decision evidence to match an example's length, or invent achievements, commitments, or approval.
4. When presentation files are requested, use available presentation-generation capabilities and inspect content and layout. If only text output is possible, identify the deliverable as a script or outline. Use only authorized templates, charts, and brand assets.

Before delivery, check audience and occasion fit, factual and numerical accuracy, visible open decisions, and suitability for the requested duration. Report material gaps separately and keep internal preference analysis out of audience-facing content.
